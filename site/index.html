The system

You give us your editing style.
We turn it into a system.

Not a template, and not a tool we hand you. Your rules, extracted from videos you have already made, then applied to every video after.

01
Style Engine

Send us reference videos. We extract how you actually edit: type, colour, caption style, hook structure, pacing, transitions, B-roll rules, sound, end cards. That becomes your Style System. Every video we produce for you looks like yours. It never looks like ours.

How it works

One call. One free sample. Then it runs.

  1. 01 Book the call.

    Thirty minutes. Bring two real numbers: how many videos a month you produce, and what they cost you. Everything after this is decided by those two numbers.

  2. 02 Free sample.

    Send links to ten videos you have already published and one piece of raw footage. We extract your rules and send back three finished clips cut in your style. Free, before any setup fee.

  3. 03 You compare.

    If the clips do not look like yours, we stop there and you owe nothing. The sample exists so you can judge this on your own material before committing to volume.

  4. 04 Setup.

    We build your full Style System, at the fee quoted on the call, and run the first production batch through it.

  5. 05 It runs.

    Weekly output, a human QA pass on every video, revisions until you approve. The more you send in a month, the less each video costs.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this going to look like our work, or like AI work?

Like yours. That is the entire product. The Style System is built from videos you have already made, so type, colour, captions, hooks and pacing are extracted from your own output rather than chosen by us. If it does not look like yours, the system has failed and we would rather you say so.

Is this AI?

Yes, underneath. You asked what it costs and how many videos it produces, so that is what we led with. There is a reasoning layer selecting the moments and applying your rules, and a person watching every video before it ships. The architecture is our problem, not yours.

What if we do not like the output?

We rebuild until you approve. Nothing reaches a client without your sign-off, and the free sample exists precisely so you can judge this on your own material before you commit to a volume you might regret.

Do we lose the client relationship?

The opposite. Your clients never deal with us. We produce under your name, in your style, and the relationship stays entirely yours.

What happens to our editors?

That is your call and we do not tell you how to staff your team. What usually happens is the repetitive cutting stops, and the same people spend their hours on the work that actually needs a person.

How long until we are live?

The setup is the slow part, because learning your style properly takes a few rounds. One format across one brand turns around quickly; five formats across twenty creators takes longer. We give you the dates before you commit, not after.

Under 20 videos a month. Should we even talk?

Probably not yet. At that volume a good freelancer usually beats us on cost, and we would be selling you something that does not fit. Put your real numbers into the calculator above and it will tell you honestly, including when the answer is no.

Is this only for podcast clips?

No. We cut short-form from any footage (podcasts, webinars, talks, talking-head and infotainment), and we make avatar videos, motion graphics, SaaS explainers and launch videos. Short-form runs on the published ladder. Project work is quoted before you commit.

Do you work with our competitors?

One production partner per niche. If we are already working with someone you compete with, we tell you on the call before you commit to anything.
